Site Plan

The Short Version

Events lead, weddings follow, and no page Google already ranks gets removed.

Today every path on the site leads with weddings: the hero video, the homepage headline, the portfolio title, and even the About page, which sits under Weddings. This plan changes the order things appear in, not the pages themselves.

  • A five-link menu

    What We Do, Gallery, About, Press, Inquire. Events come before weddings, and the Journal moves to the footer.

  • Two services pages, no new URLs

    /services becomes Event Services. /all-destination-weddings becomes Wedding Services.

  • A hero video that isn't about weddings

    A short cut of brand and corporate events replaces the wedding hero.

  • One About page

    About Us comes out of the Weddings menu. Team and process stay live and are linked from About.

  • Portfolio becomes Gallery

    Same URL. Corporate, brand and private galleries move to the front.

  • One new page, one redirect fix

    /press is new, and the /weddings redirect gets corrected. Every other URL stays the same.

Headline

Today's H1 is Destination Weddings & Events | U.S. & Italy. Reorder it to lead with events. For example:

Luxury Events & Destination Weddings

"Events" and "destination weddings" stay in the headline. "U.S." and "Italy" move to the homepage page title and meta description, so those searches still find the page.

Hero video brief
Goal
A brand or corporate client sees their kind of event in the first five seconds.
Content
Brand activations, corporate dinners and private celebrations. Rooms before guests arrive, set details, lighting changes, the team at work, Reagan directing. No couples or bridal details.
Privacy
No guests' faces. Brand names appear only where the client has approved it.
Pacing
Six to eight clips of three to four seconds each, slow camera movement, 20 to 30 seconds total. Looping and muted, with no text in the video.
Look
A warm grade in cream, antique gold, oxblood and deep green.
Phones
Keep the subject centered so it holds up in a vertical crop. Keep the file light, and check load time on a phone before launch.
Fallback
Until footage is ready, use one still from the corporate events gallery.

Needed from your teamA list of the brand, corporate and private event video you have, including any footage from the October 18 setup at Lowndes Grove.

  1. Menu labelPortfolio becomes Gallery. The URL stays the same.
  2. Tile orderCorporate & Brand, Private Parties, Destination Weddings, U.S. Weddings.
  3. H1 and page titleName events first and keep "destination weddings." For example, "Event & Wedding Design Portfolio by Reagan Events."
